Professional College of Geological Prospecting Technologies

Main academic building of the college

The Separate Structural Subdivision "Professional College of Geological Prospecting Technologies of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv" (KGRT) was established on the basis of the Kyiv Geological Prospecting Technical School (founded in 1930) and has been a structural subdivision of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv since 2012.

Our educational mission is to foster the harmonious personal development of every student, enhance their professional competencies, and build the aspiration and skills to acquire new knowledge.

The College's vision — the College of the future: the flagship of the country's professional pre-higher geological education, a functional college able to generate modern geological knowledge and ensure its transfer, creating long-term value and shaping, sustaining and developing Ukraine's subsoil resources for future generations.

The College trains specialists for the geological and extractive industries. They take up engineering and technical positions at most geological enterprises and institutions in Ukraine and beyond.

The College's greatest asset is its graduates. Over 95 years, the College has trained more than 50,000 specialists for the geological and other industries. Among them are many discoverers of mineral deposits, talented geologists, geophysicists and drillers — graduates of our College who, both in Ukraine and abroad, became renowned highly professional scientists, public figures and true masters of their craft. They still work not only in Ukraine but on other continents, discovering mineral deposits.

The College's pride is its geological museum. In an exhibition hall of 250 m² there are 54 glass display cases and 50 podiums with samples of minerals, ores, rocks and fossils. The core of the stone collection consists of minerals, rocks and ores found by students and lecturers during field practice, as well as gifts from College graduates.

Student leisure is one of the College's priority areas, as it holds a prominent place in the overall programme of educational work with young people. Today, individual educational work is highly relevant, both within the academic process and outside of class hours.

Student leisure is built around a number of clubs, sports sections and interest groups. Many cultural events have become traditional and are very popular among the College's students. The College runs sports sections (football, volleyball, basketball, table tennis) where students spend their free time. The College teams also take part in district and city competitions, where they win prizes.

Concert programmes for the Day of Knowledge, the Day of Education Workers, Student's Day, Defender's Day, International Women's Rights and Peace Day, Victory Day and Mother's Day are very popular among students.  

The College has a student government that provides significant help to students and lecturers in running events and resolving issues of social, cultural, academic and sporting life. 

Dormitory for college and university students

The College has academic and sports buildings, training workshops, a canteen, a dormitory, a training ground, a geological museum, a library with a reading room, as well as classrooms and laboratories equipped with modern apparatus and equipment. The College's computer centre is equipped with modern computers and has Internet access.

The College is a collective member of the Union of Geologists of Ukraine, the Union of Drillers of Ukraine, the Ukrainian Society for Nature Conservation, the Ukrainian Mineralogical Society and the International Association for Scientific, Technical and Business Cooperation in Geophysical Research and Borehole Operations.

Today the Professional College of Geological Prospecting Technologies of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv is the only educational institution in Ukraine that trains professional junior bachelors in all geological prospecting specialties, and is one of the leading geological educational institutions in Ukraine.



Geological prospecting training ground for college and university student practice

       Geological practice

Under the educational and professional programme "Geological Methods of Prospecting and Exploration of Mineral Deposits" the College trains professional junior bachelors who carry out geological mapping and the prospecting and exploration of mineral deposits. Graduates are awarded the educational qualification of professional junior bachelor in Earth Sciences and the professional qualification of geologist technician. 

Geological practice
Geophysical practice

Under the educational and professional programme "Exploration Geophysics and Computer Processing of Geophysical Data" the College trains professional junior bachelors who carry out the prospecting and exploration of oil and gas, coal, ore minerals, building materials and groundwater within the earth's crust. Geophysical exploration is carried out with multifunctional electronic apparatus and equipment mounted on vehicles, aircraft and ships.

Computers are used at all stages of geophysical research. Modern universal computer technologies are used in the computer processing of geophysical data. Graduates are awarded the educational qualification of professional junior bachelor in Earth Sciences and the professional qualification of geophysicist technician. 

Practical work in the engineering geology laboratory

Under the educational and professional programme "Hydrogeological and Engineering-Geological Surveys for Water Supply and Construction" the College trains professional junior bachelors who research and search for groundwater, assess soils for construction, and perform engineering-geological surveys. Graduates are awarded the educational qualification of professional junior bachelor in Earth Sciences and the professional qualification of hydrogeologist technician.

Group of ecology students during an exam in the environmental parameter measurement laboratory

Under the educational and professional programme "Applied Ecology" the College trains professional junior bachelors in monitoring the state of the environment and the use of natural resources. Graduates are awarded the educational qualification of professional junior bachelor in Ecology and the professional qualification of ecology technician.

Drilling practice at the college training ground

Under the educational and professional programme "Drilling of Boreholes for Solid Minerals and Water" the College trains professional junior bachelors in drilling boreholes for solid minerals and water and for engineering-geological surveys in construction. Graduates are awarded the educational qualification of professional junior bachelor in Mining and the professional qualifications of drilling-rig worker and driller.

Students performing laboratory work

Under the educational and professional programme "Maintenance and Repair of Geophysical, Radio-Electronic Equipment and Computer Hardware" the College trains professional junior bachelors in the maintenance and repair of electronic geophysical apparatus and radio-electronic and computer hardware. Additional professional knowledge and skills are provided in the popular fields of computer hardware repair and the administration of computer networks and operating systems.

Students learn to repair various types of radio-electronic equipment and additionally acquire the skills of professional servicing of computer systems. Graduates are awarded the educational qualification of professional junior bachelor in Mechanical Engineering and the professional qualification of mechatronics technician.


Automobile-transport students at a lecture on vehicle repair and maintenance

Under the educational and professional programme "Maintenance and Repair of Vehicles and Engines" the College trains professional junior bachelors for production, technical and organizational work related to the maintenance of vehicles and engines. Graduates are awarded the educational qualification of professional junior bachelor in Automobile Transport. 

History of the College

1930-1941

On 31 July 1930, the Kyiv Geological Prospecting Technical School (KGRT) was founded by order of the Main Geological Prospecting Directorate of the Supreme Council of the National Economy of the USSR, on the basis of a topographic vocational school.

The technical school began training specialists for the geological industry in four specialties: geological prospecting, hydrogeological, drilling and topographic.

KGRT was located at 12 Zhertv Revolyutsii St. (now Mykhailivska) and occupied one floor of the building. Four other educational institutions — two institutes and two technical schools — were located in the same building. Classes were held in two shifts, starting at 16:00. About 150 students studied at the school. At first there was no educational base apart from a small library, and no full-time lecturers. The institution was taking shape: classrooms were created, the library was stocked with literature, and the teaching staff was formed.

In 1934 the first graduation of geological specialists took place. That same year the building at 12 Zhertv Revolyutsii St. was handed over to the Institute of Red Professorship, relocated from Kharkiv to the capital of the Ukrainian SSR. The school's staff made great efforts to secure the right to move to new premises of the Industrial Workers' Faculty at 23 Chervonoarmiiska St. (now Velyka Vasylkivska), occupying 6 rooms on the ground floor. This made it possible to begin the 1934-35 academic year with six first-year groups of the new intake.

Some time later the school's classroom stock was increased by four more rooms, which made it possible to hold classes in two shifts with the full complement of students.

The school was renamed the geological-hydro-geodesic school, resulting in new specialties: mine surveying and aerial-phototopographic. The number of students was about 500. The number of full-time lecturers was about 20. Physics, geodesy and mineralogy classrooms were created.

A drilling rig was built in the courtyard, which facilitated practical training in manual drilling. The school's library was located at 4 Taras Shevchenko Boulevard, on the premises of the geological trust.

The affairs of the Kyiv Geological Prospecting Technical School improved year by year. The annual graduation of young specialists was about 100, and the number of students was replenished by new intakes (5-6 groups) of people who had completed seven-year school. Even then the school gained great popularity in Ukraine and other union republics. The number of applicants for geological specialties grew every year. Students did their practical training across the entire territory of the Soviet Union, and after graduation worked in various parts of the country. 

Between 1934 and 1941 the school graduated more than 1,000 specialists.

1941-1942

At the beginning of the Second World War, a significant number of the school's lecturers and students went to the front. KGRT was evacuated to Semipalatinsk (Kazakhstan), where the training of specialists for the country's geological industry continued; a geological prospecting school established on the basis of the Kyiv one still operates there today.

1943-1948

After the liberation of Kyiv in 1943, work began to restore the Kyiv Geological Prospecting Technical School. The school resumed its work in the premises of School No. 30 in Kyiv at 64 Saksahanskoho St. on a rental basis, holding classes in a third shift — from 20:00. The premises of secondary School No. 136 on Zhylianska St. were also used on a rental basis.

In 1945, after the end of the Second World War, students whose studies had been interrupted by the war returned to the school. As the student body grew, the problem of classroom space became acute. The need for geological personnel grew with the development of the country's economy. The government decided to build a student campus for the Kyiv Geological Prospecting Technical School. 

In 1947, construction of the academic building and dormitory began on Cherepanova Hill.

1949-1968

On 1 September 1949 the school began classes in its own academic building at 9 Henri Barbusse St. (now Vasylia Tiutiunnyka), in the part whose construction had been completed by then.

In the early 1950s a whole student campus was put into operation, including an academic building, a dormitory, a canteen, training workshops with carpentry, turning-milling and metalworking shops and a forge, as well as garage boxes for vehicles and geological prospecting equipment.

From 1950 to 1957, one-year courses operated at the Kyiv Geological Prospecting Technical School, on which about 1,500 narrowly specialized technicians were trained.

In 1952 a part-time (correspondence) form of study was opened for young people who sought to gain the profession of a pioneer of the earth's depths without leaving work.


From 1957, the training of specialists began for countries that had embarked on the path of independent development after freeing themselves from colonialism. At that time we were the first among vocational-technical institutions in Kyiv where foreign students began to study. The school trained about 500 specialists for countries of Asia, Africa and Latin America.

In 1967 a training ground was opened in the Zhytomyr region for practical training in topography, drilling and mining, and geophysical, hydrogeological and engineering-geological surveys.

By September 1968, another dormitory for 480 places was built for visiting students.

1969-1989

In September 1970 an extension to the academic building with 8 classrooms was put into operation. A student buffet-cafeteria was located on the ground floor of the extension.

1977 is notable for the completion of a new academic building and sports complex. The new and old academic buildings were connected by passages, and the total area of academic premises exceeded 14,800 m².

From this time, classes were held in a single shift, which improved extracurricular and educational work: amateur arts, technical creativity and various club activities. The sports complex put into operation made it possible to improve the work of sports sections for the physical training needed by geologists on field expeditions and parties.

A unique research borehole was built in the laboratory of geophysical well-logging methods. Its depth and equipment allow practical and laboratory classes to be held in classroom conditions that are as close as possible to production conditions, and to calibrate logging apparatus.

On 1 September 1986 the school's geological museum was opened. The museum's holdings number more than three thousand samples of minerals and rocks from deposits in various regions of Ukraine and the world, and more than 300 items in the palaeontological collection. The museum hosts classes in mineralogy, petrography, mineral resources, and general and historical geology, as well as excursions for students and schoolchildren.

Since 1987, a special department for the retraining of mid-level personnel has operated at the school, on a full-time basis, to train people in the operation, maintenance and repair of complex geophysical apparatus. Lecturers from technical schools of a geological profile from Laos, Mongolia and Vietnam also undergo internships there. 

Cooperation agreements with the Vietnamese polytechnic, the northern and southern geological prospecting schools of the DRV, and the Zawodowa geological school of Wrocław (Poland) are being successfully implemented.

In various years the school's lecturers provided assistance in the prospecting and exploration of mineral deposits and in training geological specialists in Algeria, Vietnam, China, Laos, Iran, Nigeria, Ethiopia and other countries. For many years, lecturers of geological technical schools from developing countries undertook internships at the school.

The Kyiv Geological Prospecting Technical School was subordinated to the Ministry of Geology of the Soviet Union, and later to the Ministry of Geology of Ukraine.

The College's academic building is one of the benchmarks of post-war architecture. And the stairs leading up to it are still popular among filmmakers. A frame from the film "The Queen of the Gas Station": it was filmed on the stairs of the geological prospecting college.

During Ukraine's independence

1990-2000

During Ukraine's independence, the school became a leading institution among sectoral educational establishments of the 1st-2nd levels of accreditation, and one of the best among colleges and technical schools of Ukraine in training junior specialists for the geological industry and the entire production and economic complex of Ukraine.

The school's material and technical base grew, its scientific and methodological potential strengthened, and the teaching staff's efforts were directed at the main task — training a highly qualified, competent, competitive specialist ready for active, creative, professional work.

Many graduates remember those who stood at the College's beginnings as a generation of highly intelligent, experienced lecturers who gave their knowledge and soul to their work.

A "change of generations" took place at the school. Experienced lecturers were gradually replaced by young people who took on the traditions and experience of their predecessors.

Many of that generation of the "sixtiers, seventiers and eightiers" and the early 1990s are distinguished people, the "golden fund" of our College, heads of departments and lecturers whose achievements and awards are hard to list.

The school opened the path to science and to the highest levels of state and economic governance for many of its students. Among the school's graduates are people's deputies of Ukraine, heads of industries, large enterprises, scientific institutions and higher education institutions.

Since 1991, the school has paid much attention to national revival and the expansion of educational activity, namely:

- the opening of new specialties such as "Computer Processing of Geophysical Data", "Records Management", "Economics and Planning of Geological Prospecting Work", "Accounting and Auditing", "Maintenance and Repair of Vehicles and Engines", "Maintenance and Repair of Electronic Computing Equipment in Geophysical Apparatus", and "Environmental Protection in Geological Prospecting Work and Rational Use of Natural Resources";

- the opening of preparation of foreign citizens for admission to educational institutions on a contract basis — one of the first among vocational-technical institutions in Ukraine.

Cooperation with organizations of the geological industry, both in Ukraine and in other countries, is expanding. There is effective cooperation with the South Aral Hydrogeological Trust of the "Turkmengeologiya" association. Lecturers and students of the school carried out geological and hydrogeological surveying using geophysical methods to identify freshwater lenses in the Northern Karakum (1990-1993).

In 1992 a branch of the school was opened in the city of Dashoguz, Turkmenistan. Full-time admission was organized in the specialties: "Geology, Prospecting and Exploration of Mineral Deposits"; "Hydrogeology and Engineering Geology"; "Technology and Techniques of Exploration of Mineral Deposits". The school's lecturers provided educational and methodological assistance in organizing the educational process.

In 1994, the retraining of personnel from among junior specialists began, in accordance with the order of the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine "On the organization of a special department for the retraining of personnel at the Kyiv Geological Prospecting Technical School".

The tradition of professional methodological work continues. The College's lecturers are the authors of more than 60 textbooks and study guides published at various times; among them Anokhina I.D., Bashlyk S.M., Bratslavskyi Y.A., Brevda N.Y., Horiachko I.V., Zavorotko Yu.M., Zahybailo H.T., Zaiets O.P., Kamzist Zh.S., Kovalenko A.V., Korotkykh I.V., Koshelev I.M., Levites Ya.M., Luzan V.V., Liukshyn V.S., Nahlia V.V., Ovchynnikov L.I., Petelko O.F., Rublevska L.V., Sapfirov H.M., Frolov O.F., Hontarev Yu.F. and others.

In 1999 a modern computer training centre was formed. 

Today the computer centre has been updated with current computer hardware, software and modern multimedia systems and equipment, and computer networks. The software of the computer centre's laboratories enables students to master modern computerized technologies for processing geological-geophysical, ecological and hydrogeological data.


2001-2012

Since 2001 the school has been subordinated to the Ministry of Education and Science, Youth and Sport of Ukraine. The school is a leading higher educational institution of the 1st-2nd levels of accreditation for the development of State education standards in the field of "Geology". The administration and lecturers of the school take an active part in the work of the Council of Directors and city methodological associations of higher educational institutions of the 1st-2nd levels of accreditation. 

In 2005, for significant achievements in educating the young generation and training personnel for the geological industry, the school's staff was awarded an Honorary Diploma of the Cabinet of Ministers of Ukraine.

In 2010, for services in training specialists for the exploration of Ukraine's subsoil, the State Geological Service and the Central Committee of the Trade Union of Workers of Geology, Geodesy and Cartography awarded the school the departmental distinction "V.I. Luchytskyi Medal".

From 2012 to the present

In October 2012 the school was merged into Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv and reorganized into the College of Geological Prospecting Technologies.

In 2019 the College gained the status of an institution of professional pre-higher education.

By order of the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine of 19 August 2020 No. 1072, the College was renamed the Separate Structural Subdivision "Professional College of Geological Prospecting Technologies of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv". 

Over more than 95 years of history, not only the institution's name changed but also the names of the specialties. Generations and values changed. New students and new staff arrived — those who chose the geological profession, who absorbed all the theoretical knowledge and practical skills and went out across the homeland in search of mineral deposits.

Since geology is a complex science that requires deep, comprehensive knowledge, the College trains geophysicist technicians, hydrogeologist technicians, ecology technicians, drilling technicians, equipment operation and repair technicians, vehicle repair mechanics and fitters, and professional junior bachelors in geological tourism.

Indeed, the prospecting and exploration of mineral deposits is impossible at the modern level without knowledge of geophysics and computer data processing. Likewise, knowledge of hydrogeology and ecology is essential for the exploration and extraction of mineral resources.

In other words, all the professions our College offers are connected with the geological branch of the national economy.

In addition, a deep analysis of ensuring the rational and integrated use of mineral and raw-material resources is needed, as are new approaches and the study of global analogues for the use of environmentally clean technologies of mineral extraction and the protection of the environment from man-made pollution.

Therefore, the opening of new professions at the College, or the improvement of existing ones, is possible.

The essence remains unchanged — training specialists who are competitive in any economic conditions. The material and technical base is constantly updated in line with the demands of today. 

Today the College is one of the leading educational institutions training professional junior bachelors in the geological field. In its material, technical, staffing and methodological support of the educational process, the institution meets the demands of today.

The College's material base is growing and its scientific and methodological potential is strengthening. The teaching staff's efforts are directed at the main task — training a highly qualified, competent, competitive specialist ready for active, creative, professional work.

The College is a collective member of the Union of Geologists of Ukraine, the Union of Drillers of Ukraine, the Ukrainian Society for Nature Conservation, the Ukrainian Mineralogical Society and the International Association for Scientific, Technical and Business Cooperation in Geophysical Research and Borehole Operations.

Coat of arms of Taras Shevchenko
National University of Kyiv

The coat of arms of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv was designed in 1994 to mark the 160th anniversary of the university's founding. The graphic work was done by the artist Serhii Bieliaiev.

At the centre of the heraldic composition is the university's main building — the Red Building — a symbol of fundamental university education. To the left is depicted the Archangel Michael, the heavenly patron of the land of Kyiv, and to the right is Saint Volodymyr the Great, Equal-to-the-Apostles, patron of knowledge and tireless scientific inquiry, whose name the university bore until 1919.

The year of the university's founding — 1834 — is the central idea of the composition. The golden ribbons with the inscription "Utilitas, Honor et Gloria" (lit. "Benefit, honour and glory"), which was the motto of the Order of Saint Volodymyr, define the priorities of the historical development of Shevchenko's university.

The composition of the coat of arms is crowned with a narrow red ring bearing the inscription "Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv".

Coat of arms of the Separate Structural Subdivision
"Professional College of Geological Prospecting Technologies
of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv"

The coat of arms of the Separate Structural Subdivision "Professional College of Geological Prospecting Technologies of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv" was designed in 2010 to mark the 80th anniversary of the College's founding.

At the centre of the heraldic composition is a circle coloured blue, since the ocean makes up the larger part of the entire surface of the planet EARTH.


Within the circle are inscribed compass arrows oriented in the latitudinal and meridional directions and two crossed geological hammers. "Mente et Malleo!" — with mind and hammer.

The central idea of the composition is the historical abbreviation KGRT, year of founding — 1930.

The composition of the coat of arms is crowned by a narrow yellow ring, which symbolizes the third planet from the sun of the Solar System.
