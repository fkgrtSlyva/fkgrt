REGISTRATION OF ELECTRONIC CABINETS AND SUBMISSION OF APPLICATIONS

Applicants register their application through their personal electronic cabinet, except in cases where an applicant is unable to register an electronic cabinet or submit an application in electronic form.

To submit an application in electronic form, an applicant must register a Personal Electronic Cabinet of the Applicant on the website of the Unified State Electronic Database on Education.

The document on prior education must be entered before the registration of the applicant's first application on the corresponding basis of admission.

An application in electronic form is registered by the applicant by completing the online form and is reviewed by the admissions committee of the institution in the manner established by law.

To assist applicants in registering a personal electronic cabinet and submitting applications in electronic form, a consultation centre of the admissions committee has been established.

Applicants can receive assistance at the college's consultation centre at: 9 Vasylia Tiutiunnyka St. (former Henri Barbusse), Kyiv, 03150.

Admission deadlines for the professional junior bachelor

Persons eligible to apply for the professional junior bachelor (full-time study) fall into three categories:

  • those entering on the basis of basic general secondary education;
  • those entering on the basis of complete general or specialized secondary education;
  • other categories (those entering on the basis of the educational-qualification level of a skilled worker, and others).

The admission deadlines differ for each of these categories.

The deadlines also differ for applicants entering a form of study other than full-time and dual (i.e. those planning to study part-time / by correspondence).

Admission on the basis of basic secondary education  (Stages of admission on the basis of 9 grades)

Registration of applicants' electronic cabinets and uploading of certain documents begins 25 June and ends 20 October.

Applications and documents are accepted from 1 to 14 July (until 18:00).

There may be an additional acceptance of documents for applicants to the contract (fee-paying) form of study, subject to additional enrolment no later than 20 October.

Creative competitions and individual interviews are held from 15 to 23 July.

Creative competitions during additional enrolment are held within the deadlines defined by the institution's Admission Rules.

Creative competitions are held in a single round. They apply to college specialties whose admission is considered on the basis of basic or complete general secondary education, according to the level of creative and/or physical abilities of the applicants.

No earlier than 14:00 on 23 July and no later than 12:00 on 25 July, the rating list of applicants entering on the basis of entrance assessments (in particular interviews and creative competitions) is published, indicating those recommended for enrolment. It is formed on the basis of the competitive score for each competitive offer, notifying applicants whether or not they have obtained the right to receive professional pre-higher education funded by the state budget.

By 12:00 on 29 July, budget-funded applicants who received a recommendation for enrolment must fulfil the enrolment requirements.

Persons who received a recommendation for budget enrolment but did not fulfil the enrolment requirements by 12:00 on 29 July cannot be enrolled on that competitive offer.

After applicants fulfil the enrolment requirements, the lists of applicants recommended for enrolment are updated.

Based on their position in the rating list, applicants may be given recommendations for enrolment to state- or regional-order places, if such places become available after the lists are updated, no later than 20:00 on 29 July.

Applicants who received a recommendation for enrolment after the lists were updated must fulfil the enrolment requirements for a state- or regional-order place by 18:00 on 31 July.

Enrolment takes place:

  • for budget-funded applicants – no later than 18:00 on 1 August;
  • for contract applicants – no later than 4 August;
  • in the case of additional enrolment – no later than 20 October.

No later than 8 August, transfer to vacant budget places from the contract form of study is carried out.

Admission on the basis of complete general secondary education  (Stages of admission on the basis of 11 grades)

Registration of applicants' electronic cabinets and uploading of certain documents begins 01 July.

The document on prior education must be entered before the registration of the applicant's first application on the corresponding basis of admission. An applicant may add several documents on prior education to their electronic cabinet, drawn from the EDEBO Register of Education Documents, in order to register applications on other bases of admission up to the day before the registration deadline.

Registration of applications and documents begins 5 July and ends:

  • at 18:00 on 22 July – for persons entering on the basis of an interview or creative competition;
  • at 18:00 on 02 August – for persons entering solely on the results of the NMT (National Multi-subject Test).

Creative competitions and interviews are held from 23 July to 01 August inclusive.

Rating lists of applicants for each competitive offer, indicating those recommended for enrolment, are formed and published no earlier than 5 August and no later than 12:00 on 07 August.

Applicants who received recommendations must fulfil the enrolment requirements for state- or regional-order places by 18:00 on 11 August.

Enrolment of applicants to budget places takes place no later than 12:00 on 14 August, and to contract places no later than 12:00 on 30 August.

Transfer of persons enrolled on contract to vacant budget places will be completed no later than 26 August.

Additional intake 

Institutions have the right to organize an additional intake of students to the contract form of study (no more than two consecutive application sessions).

The duration of each application session is no less than two weeks and no more than three calendar months.

Creative competitions during an institution's additional intake are held within the deadlines defined by the Admission Rules.

Additional enrolment must be carried out no later than 20 October.
